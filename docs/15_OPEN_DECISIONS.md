## 1. Document Purpose

This document tracks all open architectural decisions, pending requirements clarifications, trade-off analyses, and formal Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) for the **NIET Dissertation Management System**.

## 2. Information Contained in this Document

This document contains:
- **Decision Tracking Index**: Table of active, proposed, accepted, and rejected decision items.
- **Architectural Decision Records (ADR Format)**:
  - **Title & Identifier**: Unique decision number and topic.
  - **Context & Problem Statement**: Background and rationale for the decision.
  - **Options Considered**: List of viable approaches with pros and cons.
  - **Decision & Rationale**: The chosen approach and justification.
  - **Status**: `PROPOSED`, `ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED`, or `DEPRECATED`.
  - **Consequences & Impact**: Positive, negative, and neutral trade-offs resulting from the decision.
